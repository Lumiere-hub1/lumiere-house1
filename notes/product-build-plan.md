# Product build slice

The current product surfaces already cover the main Today, Growth, Client Care, Content Studio, Automate, Connect, onboarding, approval, and quality flows. The most material customer-facing gap is information architecture: Results exists as a persisted analytics screen but is hidden behind Settings rather than being a first-class destination.

The next safe slice promotes Results into the primary tab bar and extends the existing analytics screen with a compact results lens showing target, forecast, assumptions, and actual results. It uses the existing workspace-scoped goal and analytics APIs, keeps forecast unavailable until evidence exists, and does not infer revenue or fabricate provider data. No new database tables or external credentials are required.
